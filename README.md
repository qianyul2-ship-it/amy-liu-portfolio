# Amy Liu | Data Visualization Portfolio

Welcome to my portfolio for **Telling Stories with Data** at Carnegie Mellon University.

## About Me

I'm Amy, a graduate student in the Master of Information Systems Management (MISM) program at Carnegie Mellon University. I'm interested in product management, artificial intelligence, and using technology to solve practical problems.

Through internships and team projects, I've worked on AI-powered product experiences, user research, and growth strategy. I enjoy understanding what users need and using data to guide product decisions.

## What I Hope to Learn

In Telling Stories with Data, I hope to learn how to turn complex information into clear, engaging visual stories. I'm particularly interested in choosing the right charts, designing for different audiences, and communicating insights without overwhelming readers.

After graduation, I plan to pursue product management roles, especially in AI products and growth. I hope to apply data storytelling to better explain user problems, evaluate product performance, and share recommendations with teams.
