## Government Debt Across Major Economies

### Government Debt Diverged After 2008

This visualization compares general government debt as a percentage of GDP in Japan, the United States, the United Kingdom, France, and Germany from 1995 to 2019. The line chart highlights differences in debt trends, particularly following the 2008 financial crisis.

Japan maintained the highest debt-to-GDP ratio, while the United States and the United Kingdom experienced substantial increases after 2008. Germany followed a different trajectory, with debt declining in later years.

[View the interactive Tableau visualization](https://public.tableau.com/views/GovernmentDebtAcrossMajorEconomies/Sheet2)

[View the original heatmap](https://public.tableau.com/views/GovernmentDebtAcrossMajorEconomies/Sheet1)

**Data source:** OECD, General Government Debt (% of GDP), 1995–2019. https://www.oecd.org/en/data.html
